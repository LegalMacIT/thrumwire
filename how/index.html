How Thrum researches

What Thrum looks for, where it looks and how far back — for every beat. This page is built from the same settings Thrum uses, so it always matches.

Rules for every beat

  • Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, searches the web for the most viral or consequential stories on each beat — usually three per run.
  • It searches worldwide and in many languages, prefers primary sources and reputable outlets, and writes in English.
  • A story is only included if its source could be read; every briefing links to that source.
  • Stories already covered are skipped, so the same story doesn't return.
  • Each story gets an impact score from 0 to 100: Critical 85–100, High 65–84, Medium 40–64, Low below 40.
  • Our publisher reads what Thrum finds and selects what is published. The research and writing are Claude's, and every post says so.
